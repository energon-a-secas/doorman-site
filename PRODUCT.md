# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Builders comparing practical service combinations before copying a product workflow with an AI coding tool. They want to recognize brands, scan tables, compare costs and caveats, and copy a useful prompt.

## Product Purpose

A compact stack cookbook: pick a recipe, swap ingredients, compare prices and tradeoffs, and take the prompt.

## Brand Personality

Practical and candid, with the existing Doorman cooking metaphor and Neorgon identity. Preserve the original visual character and service brand icons.

## Design Principles

1. The original single-page cookbook is the approved baseline.
2. Keep compact branded service rows and tabular cost/model comparisons.
3. Refine readability, alignment, responsive behavior, focus and useful transitions.
4. Keep existing information and interactions discoverable without adding more screens.
5. Label assumptions honestly; preserve service details and prices.

## Anti-references

The rejected multi-view planner, persistent summary sidebar, generic scope templates, long service essays replacing compact rows, or another new information architecture.

## Decision source

Latest user direction: “Go back to the original design to visualize things as tables and the brands … just focus on UI enhancements over the original design.” This supersedes the previous redesign plan and its recipe-first revision.
